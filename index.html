Hofstede Cultural Orientation Quiz

Welcome!
The Hofstede’s Cultural Dimensions Test helps you explore how cultural values influence the way we think, work, and relate to others. It looks at areas such as power distribution, individual vs. group orientation, and how we plan for the future.

This version is a personal reflection tool, not a comparison between countries. Think of it as a snapshot of your current cultural tendencies — a starting point for self-awareness and growth.

Remember: There are no right or wrong answers. Your results may change depending on your mood, situation, or context. Use them to better understand yourself and to appreciate the different ways others might see the world.
